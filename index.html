WealthView: finding and fixing an 18% drop in active users

A fictional wealth-management app lost 18% of its monthly active users in four months, and leadership asked what to fix first. I traced the drop to an onboarding redesign that broke identity verification on Android, ranked fixes with RICE, tested the top one with an A/B test, and recommended a staged rollout with fraud monitoring.
